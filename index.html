ShareQtyBuyNowP/LSignal

To pull holdings straight from your broker, set up the small free proxy in the broker-proxy.js file (see README) and paste its address in Settings. Otherwise enter holdings here or import a CSV (symbol,exchange,qty,price). Everything stays on your device.

Live prices

The app works offline with sample (demo) prices. For real prices, paste a free API key from twelvedata.com (covers NSE, BSE, NASDAQ, NYSE, LSE and more; the free plan has daily limits). The key is stored only in this browser.

Broker and alerts

Alerts are checked whenever the app is open. Your broker password and keys never go in this app.

How signals are made

Each share is scored from −7 to +7 using: price vs 50-day average, 20/50-day trend, RSI, 20-day momentum, MACD and Bollinger bands. Score 3 or more = BUY (with a suggested stop-loss and target from recent volatility), −3 or less = SELL, otherwise HOLD. This is rule-based maths on price history only; it does not read news, results or company fundamentals.
